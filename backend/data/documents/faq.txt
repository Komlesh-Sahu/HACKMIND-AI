LEARNATHON 5.0 OFFICIAL FAQ

Q: Who can participate in Learnathon 5.0?
A: Learnathon 5.0 is open to all GIET University students studying in their 1st, 2nd, 3rd, or 4th year, from any academic discipline.

Q: Is Learnathon 5.0 open to students from other colleges?
A: No. This edition is exclusively for GIET University, Gunupur students.

Q: Can I form a team with students from a different branch?
A: Yes. Cross-branch teams are fully allowed and encouraged.

Q: What is the team size for Learnathon 5.0?
A: Each team must have a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 4 members.

Q: Is there a registration fee?
A: No. Registration for Learnathon 5.0 is completely free.

Q: Is this an online or offline event?
A: Learnathon 5.0 is a fully offline, on-campus event held at GIET University, Gunupur, from 27th–30th August 2026.

Q: What's the prize pool?
A: The total prize pool for Learnathon 5.0 is ₹1,50,000, distributed across winners, runners-up, and special category awards.

Q: What do I need to bring on event day?
A: Bring your laptop, charger, college ID card, and registration confirmation email.

Q: Can I choose my own problem statement?
A: Yes. Teams can pick a problem statement from any of the listed domains once problem statements are released on Day 1.

Q: When will I get confirmation after registering?
A: Confirmation emails will be sent to all registered teams by 12th August 2026.

Q: What if my team doesn't have a fixed idea before the event?
A: That is completely fine. Day 1 includes a dedicated ideation phase right after problem statements are released.

Q: Who do I contact for more information?
A: Contact Dr. Sibo Prasad Patro or Dr. Raghvendra Kumar, or email raghvendra@giet.edu.
