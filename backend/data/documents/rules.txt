LEARNATHON 5.0 OFFICIAL RULES

1. Teams must consist of 2 to 4 members.

2. Cross-branch teams are allowed, but all team members must be currently enrolled students of GIET University, Gunupur.

3. Each team must register through the official registration process before the team registration deadline.

4. Participants must choose one problem statement domain and one technology track at the time of registration/submission.

5. All code and solutions must be built during the hackathon window.

6. Pre-built projects are not permitted.

7. Open-source libraries and APIs are allowed with proper attribution.

8. Plagiarism, code theft, or any form of malpractice may lead to immediate disqualification.

9. Teams must carry a valid college ID card for verification at check-in.

10. The decision of the judging panel will be final and binding.

11. Organizers reserve the right to modify rules, schedules, or problem statements if required.

12. Participants are expected to maintain discipline and follow the code of conduct throughout the event on campus.
