LEARNATHON 5.0 — EVENT INFORMATION

Event Name and Dates:
Learnathon 5.0 will be held from 27th August 2026 to 30th August 2026 at GIET University, Gunupur.

Event Mode:
Learnathon 5.0 is a fully offline and on-campus event held at GIET University, Gunupur.

Eligibility:
Learnathon 5.0 is open to GIET University students studying in 1st, 2nd, 3rd, or 4th year from any academic discipline.

External College Participation:
Students from other colleges are not eligible for Learnathon 5.0.

Team Size:
Each Learnathon 5.0 team must have a minimum of 2 and maximum of 4 members.

Cross-Branch Teams:
Cross-branch teams are allowed and encouraged.

Registration Fee:
Registration for Learnathon 5.0 is free.

Prize Pool:
The Learnathon 5.0 prize pool is ₹1,50,000, distributed across winners, runners-up, and special category awards.

Items Participants Should Bring:
Participants should bring a laptop, charger, college ID card, and registration confirmation email.

Contact:
For more information, contact Dr. Sibo Prasad Patro or Dr. Raghvendra Kumar.
